## Short bio

Help build Kudium Browser: a free, open-source browser that brings tabs, AI, project files and terminals into one focused workspace.

## Introduction

We're building Kudium Browser to help you browse, organise and work without constantly switching apps.

Keep work and personal website logins in separate tab groups. Bring AI, project files, an editor, scripts and a terminal beside the web, in one workspace.

Kudium Browser is free and open source, and is still in development. Sponsorship helps fund development, testing, maintenance and release infrastructure.

You can support us monthly or with a one-time contribution. Contributions support the project; they do not purchase paid features, priority support or a guaranteed release date.

You can also help by reporting issues, testing builds or contributing pull requests at https://github.com/kudium/web-browser.

Thank you for helping build Kudium.
